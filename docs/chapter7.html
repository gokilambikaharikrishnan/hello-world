UPS & Home Energy Storage

Six chapters of theory meet one chapter of application. UPS systems demand instant response, always-ready float states, and deep discharge recovery. Home ESS demands fifteen-year calendar life, indoor safety, and user-visible accuracy. LFP was not a compromise choice for these applications — it was engineered for them. This final chapter closes the loop from LFP chemistry to the inverter on your wall.
