[INTRO]

Welcome to Chapter 2 of the LFP Battery Management System Knowledge Base.

This chapter is called: Cell Electrochemistry and BMS Algorithms.

Chapter 1 told you which battery chemistry to use and why. Chapter 2 goes inside
the cell and answers the harder question: what is actually happening in there,
and how does the Battery Management System track it, protect against it,
and keep the pack healthy for years?

By the end of this chapter, you will understand four things clearly.

First: the exact chemical process that stores and releases energy inside an LFP cell
— and why that process creates a voltage curve unlike any other battery chemistry.

Second: why the BMS cannot simply read the battery's voltage to know how full it is
— and what it does instead.

Third: how cells age, what kills them, and what the BMS can do to slow that down.

And fourth: how all of this connects to real hardware — real protection limits,
real SOC algorithms, real commissioning steps on a real bench.

Everything in this chapter uses real numbers from our reference design:
a sixteen-cell, two-eighty amp-hour lithium iron phosphate pack,
running at forty-eight volts nominal, storing about fourteen point three kilowatt-hours.

Let's start at the very beginning — inside the cell.
